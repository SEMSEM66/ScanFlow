# ScanFlow — App Store Screenshot Guidelines & Composer Specification

This directory defines the screenshot assets, framing specifications, and copywriting for ScanFlow’s **Apple App Store** product page in both German and English.

---

## 1. Required Apple App Store Screenshot Dimensions

| Target Device Tier | Screen Size | Resolution (Portrait) | Aspect Ratio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **iPhone 16 Pro Max** (Primary 6.9") | 6.9 inch | **1320 × 2868 px** | 19.5:9 |
| **iPhone 15 Pro Max / 14 Pro Max** (6.7") | 6.7 inch | **1290 × 2796 px** | 19.5:9 |
| **iPhone 11 Pro Max / XS Max** (6.5") | 6.5 inch | **1242 × 2688 px** | 19.5:9 |

*Note: App Store Connect accepts 1290 × 2796 px or 1320 × 2868 px for modern iPhones and automatically downscales for smaller displays.*

## 2. Screenshot Sequence & Localized Copywriting

## 3. Style & Layout Guidelines

- **Background:** Clean warm ivory gradient (`#FBF9F5` to `#F0EDE6`) or deep midnight (`#0A0A0C` to `#16161A`) matching the app theme tokens.
- **Typography:** SF Pro Display Bold for headlines, SF Pro Text Regular for subtitles.
- **Accent:** ScanFlow vibrant blue (`#0066FF`).
- **Device Frame:** Minimalist bezel mockup with natural drop shadow (40px blur, 15% opacity).
